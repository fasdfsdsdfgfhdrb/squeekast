02

Three steps in OBS

Works in OBS, Streamlabs and anything that takes a browser source.

STEP 01

Add a browser source

Sources → + → Browser. Paste the URL from the builder above.

STEP 02

Size it to the mode

Wheel 420×420, ticker 1280×92, alert 1280×720. Leave “Shutdown source when not visible” off so the alert keeps listening.

STEP 03

Leave transparency on

Pick Transparent background and OBS composites it over your scene with no chroma key. Switch to Solid for a full-frame panel.
